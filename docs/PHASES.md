# Delivery map

## Phase 1 — identity and shared care space

Authentication, refresh rotation, member-scoped authorization, care circles, dashboard, EF migration, seed data, Docker Compose, and health checks.

## Phase 2 — daily care coordination

Shared tasks, medication schedules and administration logs, appointments, and background due-item scanning.

## Phase 3 — continuity and communication

Persistent care-team conversations, message history, structured handoffs, and recipient acceptance.

## Phase 4 — assisted coordination

Independent AI service for summaries and handoff drafts, visible disclaimers, review-before-share behavior, and a dedicated AI Assistant mobile screen.
